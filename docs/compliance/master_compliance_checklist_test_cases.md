## 2. Core Functional Test Cases

### Case ID: `IRC-P01` — Register B2C Sales Invoice Without Buyer TIN and With VAT
- **Objective**: Verify registration and printing of a VAT-inclusive B2C sales invoice with multiple VAT rates (VAT0, VAT15, VATEX).
- **Execution Steps**:
  1. Prepare sales document in system with items carrying `VAT0` (0%), `VAT15` (15%), and `VATEX` (Exempt).
  2. Buyer TIN is omitted; Buyer legal name/KID provided.
  3. Issue invoice via `POST /api/v1/invoices`.
  4. Print full layout invoice and thermal receipt.
- **Verification**:
  1. Scan printed QR code using official MoR mobile app.
  2. Verify IRN and AckDate generated by MoR gateway.
- **Expected Result**: Successfully registered; valid IRN and scan-able QR returned; correct tax breakdown calculated.

---

### Case ID: `IRC-P02` — Register B2B Sales Invoice with Buyer TIN and Legal Name
- **Objective**: Verify registration of a B2B sales invoice with mandatory Buyer TIN, legal name, discounts, excise tax, and withholding.
- **Execution Steps**:
  1. Submit B2B invoice with Buyer TIN `0016324478`, legal name, and address.
  2. Include line discounts, 10% excise tax, and transaction withholding.
  3. Issue invoice via `POST /api/v1/invoices`.
  4. Print full A4 invoice layout.
- **Verification**:
  1. QR code verified via official scanner.
  2. If buyer TIN is invalid or unformatted, system rejects transaction with clear validation error.
- **Expected Result**: Successfully registered with IRN and signed QR.

---

### Case ID: `IRC-P03` — Register Sales Receipt from a Registered Invoice
- **Objective**: Verify functionality of registering a sales receipt linked to a previously registered invoice in EIRMS.
- **Execution Steps**:
  1. Take registered invoice from `IRC-P01` or `IRC-P02`.
  2. Dispatch `POST /api/v1/receipts/sales` referencing the original invoice IRN.
  3. Receive confirmation and assigned RRN (Receipt Reference Number).
  4. Print receipt with QR code and RRN.
- **Expected Result**: Receipt linked to invoice; assigned valid RRN and scan-able QR code.

---

### Case ID: `IRC-P04` — Register Withhold Receipt from a Registered Invoice
- **Objective**: Verify registration of a withholding tax receipt linked to a previously registered invoice.
- **Execution Steps**:
  1. Identify registered invoice with withholding applicable.
  2. Submit `POST /api/v1/receipts/withholding` referencing the invoice IRN and withholding amount.
  3. Capture generated RRN and print withholding voucher.
- **Expected Result**: Withholding receipt registered; linked to original invoice IRN; assigned valid RRN.

---

### Case ID: `IRC-P06` — Register Credit Memo from a Registered Invoice
- **Objective**: Verify registration of a Credit Note / Memo issued against a previously registered invoice.
- **Execution Steps**:
  1. Submit `POST /api/v1/adjustments/credit-notes` referencing original IRN.
  2. Verify credit amount does not exceed original invoice value.
  3. Capture generated credit note IRN and print credit note layout.
- **Expected Result**: Successfully registered; references original IRN; rejected if credit exceeds original invoice total.

---

### Case ID: `IRC-P05` — Cancel Registered Invoice (Directive Art. 26)
- **Objective**: Verify processing and confirmation of invoice cancellation requests.
- **Execution Steps**:
  1. Initiate cancellation request via `POST /api/v1/cancellations` with valid reason and justification.
  2. Submit evidence if requested before 48-hour SLA expiry.
  3. Confirm cancellation approval response from MoR `/v1/cancel`.
  4. Verify invoice status transitions to `CANCELLED`.
- **Expected Result**: Invoice cancelled; status updated in back office; reprint displays `"CANCELLED"` watermark.

---

### Case ID: `IRC-P07` — Register Debit Memo from a Registered Invoice
- **Objective**: Verify registration of a Debit Note issued against an existing invoice.
- **Execution Steps**:
  1. Submit `POST /api/v1/adjustments/debit-notes` referencing original IRN with upward price adjustment.
  2. Confirm registration response from MoR and print debit memo.
- **Expected Result**: Debit note registered with distinct IRN referencing original transaction.

## 3. Negative Security & Boundary Test Cases

### Case ID: `IRC-N08` — Negative: Register B2B Invoice with Invalid Buyer TIN or Legal Name
- **Action**: Submit B2B invoice with invalid TIN `000000000` or random unformatted string.
- **Expected Result**: System halts transaction; displays structured error: `INVALID_BUYER_TIN`; prevents submission.

### Case ID: `IRC-N09` — Negative: Generate Receipt from Non-Existent or Cancelled Invoice
- **Action**: Submit sales receipt request referencing random fake IRN or a previously cancelled invoice IRN.
- **Expected Result**: System rejects request with `INVOICE_NOT_FOUND` or `CANNOT_ISSUE_RECEIPT_FOR_CANCELLED_INVOICE`; halts operation.

### Case ID: `IRC-N010` — Negative: Cancel Non-Existent or Already Cancelled Invoice
- **Action**: Submit cancellation request for non-existent IRN or re-cancel an invoice already in `CANCELLED` state.
- **Expected Result**: System rejects duplicate cancellation with `INVOICE_ALREADY_CANCELLED`; prevents duplicate submissions.

## 4. Cross-Cutting & Auxiliary Test Cases

### Case ID: `ADD-N001` — Notification Service
- **Action**: Execute invoice registration, cancellation, debit note, and credit note with buyer email & phone specified.
- **Verification**: Buyer receives SMS and email within 5 minutes containing transaction details, IRN, and action reason.
- **Expected Result**: Asynchronous notification successfully delivered.

### Case ID: `ADD-C001` — Setup & Dynamic Credential Configuration
- **Action**: Verify runtime configuration of MoR API keys, client secrets, and INSA private keys.
- **Verification**:
  1. Sensitive credentials entered dynamically via administrative API / Vault without hard-coding in source code.
  2. Only authorized administrators (`ROLE_TENANT_ADMIN`) can view/modify configuration.
  3. Environment securely stores credentials in encrypted form; inaccessible to standard operators or cashiers.
- **Expected Result**: Pass. Strict cryptographic security and secret isolation.

### Case ID: `ADD-P001` — Printing Layout & Content
- **Action**: Generate printouts from standard A4 PDF and thermal receipt layouts.
- **Verification**:
  1. Thermal (80mm) and A4 layouts contain all Article 20 VAT Reg 570 mandatory fields.
  2. High-contrast, clearly legible IRN, RRN, ack date, tax breakdown, and scan-able QR code.
- **Expected Result**: Pass. Visually appealing and compliant output.
